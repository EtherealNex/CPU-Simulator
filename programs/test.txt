        SUB CONST1
        STA POWER

        BRA RESET

        SUB CONST1
        STA REP

        BRA LOOP

        STA VAR1
        LDA CONST2
        STA REP
        BRA CHKREP

        ADD VAR1
        STA NUM
        BRA CHKREP

        OUT
        BRA INPUT

        OUT
        BRA INPUT
